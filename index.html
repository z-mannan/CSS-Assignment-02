About the Book

The Alchemist is a story about Santiago, a young shepherd who travels in search of a hidden treasure. Along the way, he discovers lessons about courage, listening to his heart, and following his dreams.

Why I Like This Book

I like this book because it encourages readers to follow their dreams and keep learning from life's experiences. Its simple storytelling and inspiring message remind me to stay curious, work hard, and believe in my goals.
